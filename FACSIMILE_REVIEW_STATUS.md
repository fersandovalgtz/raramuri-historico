Boundary review validates headword presence, article-start boundary and exact page placement. It does not by itself constitute independent linguistic or philological validation.

## Deterministic review queue

The next editorial cohort is generated by `scripts/generate_review_queue.py`. It selects the first 100 unreviewed records in source order, exhausting `medium_machine` before entering `low_machine`, and excludes every persistent ID already represented in append-only review manifests. The generated projections `data/review/next_review_queue.json` and `data/review/next_review_queue_compact.json` make cohort selection reproducible rather than manually reconstructed.
