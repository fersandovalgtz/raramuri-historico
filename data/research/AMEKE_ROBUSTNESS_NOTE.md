## Critical provenance caveat

The stronger claim of independent replication by source provenance is **not supported**. Among machine-recovered DE–RAR-only members, the context effect is 15/22 = 0.682 versus 17/85 = 0.200 in the rest (Δ = 0.482; empirical p=0.00005). Among RAR–DE-only members, the corresponding effect is only 1/9 = 0.111 versus 1/58 = 0.017 (Δ = 0.094; empirical p=0.2514). Thus the aggregate association is **provenance-sensitive**: it survives stratification, but it is not independently significant in the RAR–DE-only subset.

This distinction is essential. Stratified permutation asks whether the aggregate association survives composition controls; provenance-specific subgroup analysis asks whether the pattern independently replicates in each provenance. The former is positive; the latter is incomplete. Because much of the signal is carried by the DE–RAR residual-recovery layer, no morphological, grammatical or semantic function should be inferred from the result.
